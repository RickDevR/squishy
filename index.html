About Squishy Universe Deluxe

Welcome to the ultimate digital tactile playground! Featuring 35 ultra-realistic squishies including handmade dumplings, cactus needles, creamy butter blocks, cheeses, and adorable plush treats.

Built with advanced Web Audio API procedural synthesis and dynamic file handling. Add your own custom `.wav` or `.mp3` files named `squishy1.wav`, `squishy2.wav`, etc., and the app will instantly adopt them while automatically generating pristine, satisfying squish audio for all remaining items.
